<h2 align="center">⚡ Prompts &amp; Bypasses</h2>

<p align="center">
  One of the main parts of Byte is our growing collection of prompts.
  Models change constantly, which means that a prompt that works today may
  behave differently tomorrow. Because of this, Byte focuses on keeping its
  collection updated and organized instead of relying on a small number of
  outdated prompts.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Our collection covers a wide range of AI systems and models, with prompts
  for experimentation, behavior testing, optimization, and bypass research.
  We also include prompts intended for legitimate use cases, such as improving
  consistency, reducing unnecessary usage, refining instructions, and getting
  more useful results from an AI model.
</p>

<p align="center">
  If a particular prompt does not work for you, don't immediately assume that
  Byte is broken. AI providers frequently update their models, safety systems,
  system instructions, and surrounding infrastructure. A prompt can therefore
  stop working without anything being wrong with your setup. That's exactly
  why we maintain a large and continuously evolving collection rather than
  depending on a single method.
</p>
