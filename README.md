<h2 align="center">About Byte</h2>

<p align="center">
  Byte is a project focused on AI experimentation, prompt engineering,
  research, and user control. Our goal is to provide a constantly evolving
  collection of prompts, tools, experiments, and resources for users who want
  to explore the capabilities and limitations of modern AI systems.
</p>

<p align="center">
  We provide regularly updated, high-quality prompts for AI experimentation,
  including jailbreak and bypass research, as well as legitimate optimization
  prompts designed to improve AI workflows and make better use of available
  models and services.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Whether you are testing how a model responds to different instructions,
  experimenting with prompt techniques, optimizing an existing workflow, or
  researching the limitations of an AI system, Byte is designed to give you
  the resources to experiment in one place.
</p>

<br>

<h2 align="center">Prompts &amp; Bypasses</h2>

<p align="center">
  One of the core parts of Byte is our growing collection of prompts. AI
  models are constantly changing, which means that prompts can become
  ineffective when providers update their models, system instructions,
  moderation systems, or other underlying infrastructure.
</p>

<p align="center">
  For that reason, Byte maintains a large and continuously evolving
  collection instead of relying on a single method. Our resources cover
  different AI systems and models and include prompts for experimentation,
  behavior testing, optimization, and bypass research.
</p>

<p align="center">
  If a particular prompt does not work, that does not necessarily mean there
  is an issue with Byte. AI providers can change their systems at any time,
  causing previously effective techniques to behave differently or stop
  working entirely. We therefore aim to keep the collection updated as the
  AI landscape develops.
</p>

<br>

<h2 align="center">Byte. Launcher</h2>

<p align="center">
  Byte is more than a collection of prompts. Our launcher brings useful tools,
  resources, and Byte functionality together into a single application,
  making it easier to access the project without manually searching through
  multiple files and resources.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Depending on the current release, the launcher can provide access to prompt
  collections, utilities, configuration options, and other tools developed
  around the Byte ecosystem.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Some supported workflows can also make use of multiple API keys to
  distribute usage across available credentials. This can be useful for users
  working with API-based AI services and workflows where a single key would
  otherwise become a limitation.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Our goal is simple: give users more control over their AI workflows and
  provide the tools necessary to decide how those workflows are used.
</p>
